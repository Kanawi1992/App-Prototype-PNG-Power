EasiPay Prepaid & USSD Buying Guide

Instantly top up your electricity meter using authorized banking partners and USSD shortcodes across PNG.

*775# - bmobile / Telikom Min: K15 | Max: K50

Dial *775# on your bmobile/Telikom handset, enter your 11-digit meter number, specify the amount, and confirm.

Missing SMS Token? Dial *776# and enter your meter number to retrieve your last token directly on your phone screen.
*131# - BSP Mobile Banking Instant SMS

Dial *131#, enter your 4-digit PIN, select TopUp Services, then select Easipay. Enter your account, meter number, and amount.

*888# - Digicel Cellmoni Zero Service Fee

Dial *888#, select Option 2 (Top-up Services) → Option 2 (Easipay). Enter meter number, amount (min K15), and wallet PIN.

EasiPay Meter Indicator Light Guide

Green Light: Sufficient credit available on meter. Normal consumption.
Amber Light: Approximately 1 week of power remaining. Plan your top-up.
Red Light (Flashing): Critical low credit (~3 days remaining). Purchase units immediately.

Report fallen powerlines, transformer sparks, or generate formal fault dispatch tickets.

Fallen Powerline Emergency Protocol

  1. Stay at least 10 meters away from any fallen powerline, downed cable, or sparking transformer.
  2. Assume all wires are live and carrying lethal voltage.
  3. Keep children, pets, and bystanders completely away from the hazard zone.
  4. Note the nearest Pole Identification Stencil Number (e.g., POM-A12-04).
  5. Call PNG Power 24/7 emergency dispatch immediately on 116.
